Why we're moving to RS (Brano)

Fewer "bag full" errors
Improved moisture detection lets water evaporate, so the bag can be used longer.
Bag tab always closes
Debris will not fall out when removing the bag from the bin.
Less plastic
More environmentally friendly than IR (TPW).
Cheaper to make
Lower unit cost on the variant 71% of customers will receive.
The Plan

One product. Two variants.

We tag every customer record in Shopify (not customer facing). They order normally.

We ship the bag that fits their robot.

(There are some edge cases.)

04Timeline

What happens, and when

Aug 21, 2026
LAUNCH
Variants live. Customers tagged. 20K bag inventory buffer reached. Production and packout switch to RS (Brano) at the same time.
Aug – Sept 2026
TRANSITION
10a robots continue shipping with RS (Brano) bags. 10a hardware still supports both IR and RS. CX monitors for tag and packout issues.
October 2026
CUTOVER
Hardware 11a rolls out: IR sensor removed, firmware turns off IR sensing. ~9,500 10a robots in the field stay dual-compatible. Customers with a pre-p08d and an 11a robot need both bag types — resolved by bot ID, appended automatically for app orders and entered at checkout for web-direct orders.
05Prioritizations Needed

Three open dependencies

Together these reduce manual work and protect customers with mixed-generation robots.
Store / Web
Post-October bag selection
Customers with a pre-p08d robot and a post-October robot will need both bag types, so we can no longer infer the right bag from the account alone.

Via the app (preferred): bot ID is appended automatically. We know the robot, nothing is asked, the right bag ships.

Direct to web: bot ID becomes a field at checkout. If it is left blank we have no way to validate, and we may ship the wrong bag.
Required by October cutover
App Team
Pass bot ID via store URL
When customers tap "order accessories" in the Matic app, append their bot ID to the store URL so we can pre-fill the checkout field. Amy confirmed this is a quick effort.

After October this stops being a convenience and becomes the only path that is reliable end to end. Driving accessory orders through the app is the mitigation for web-direct blanks.
Primary mechanism post-cutover · date not yet committed
Operations Team
Household concept by October
Link multiple emails to one household via address or app login. Catches the case of a spouse ordering with a different email. Reduces — does not eliminate — cross-email errors.
Pairs with Alessandro's tag automation
HW Team · Decided
Repairs & refurbs: no bin upgrade
Pre-p08d bots in repair keep their original bin (~$100 subassembly plus labor, not economical to swap).

Replacements: the bot is fully swapped for a 10a/11a. The customer's tag flips from IR to RS automatically so their next order routes correctly (Alessandro building the automation).
Karthik confirmed
06Risks

What might still go wrong

Cross-email households
A spouse orders with a different email. We tag accounts, not households (yet), so the wrong bag may ship.
MITIGATION
CX intercepts. The household concept reduces this but will not fully eliminate it.
Mixed-generation households
After October, 11a robots only work with RS. A household with a pre-p08d robot and an 11a robot needs both bag types.
MITIGATION
Bot ID resolves it. App orders carry it automatically; web-direct orders depend on the customer filling it in.
Unknown new customers
New email plus a skipped bot ID at checkout. We cannot validate, so we may ship the wrong bag.
MITIGATION
Bot-ID-via-URL from the app helps. Web orders still need the prompt plus email follow-up.
